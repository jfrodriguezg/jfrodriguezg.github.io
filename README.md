## Case 01 — Beyond growth: Colombian food retail

**Question:** Are the fastest-growing retailers also the most profitable, and what balance-sheet pressures accompany their growth?

The case compares Éxito, D1, Ara, Olímpica and Cencosud Colombia over 2021–2025 using annual legal-entity financial statements. It is an independent historical analysis project, not employment experience.

## What the work demonstrates

Financial statement analysis, data preparation, formula-based Excel modeling, company benchmarking, reconciliation and executive communication. The downloadable model has editable inputs and a year/currency selector. Static website figures and commentary must be regenerated after model changes.

## Method and sources

Growth is nominal. USD sales enable scale comparison; ratios use COP. Net sales are distinct from total operating income. EBITDA is the supplied series and is not standardized recurring EBITDA. ROA/ROE use average balances, unlike the separate closing-balance EMIS check. Purchases are proxied by COGS for payable days.

Original source stated in the supplied files: Superintendencia Financiera for Éxito; Superintendencia de Sociedades for the other entities. Consultation/extraction channel: EMIS University. Selected Éxito 2025/2024 figures were checked against the public separate-statement PDF. A full regulatory re-extraction is not claimed. Original vendor exports and narratives are not included.

## Limitations

Different retail formats and legal-entity scopes affect comparisons. Éxito’s separate earnings include subsidiaries. D1’s asset discontinuity requires source-note review. Seven cash-flow control exceptions are documented; the Éxito closing-cash zero is resolved in the original PDF and six remain open. No cash-generation or FCF conclusion is based on those exceptions. No market-share, investment recommendation or credit rating is provided.
